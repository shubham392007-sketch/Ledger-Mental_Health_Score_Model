How it works

Four steps, no guesswork.

The same request your FastAPI endpoint handles — just wrapped in a form instead of a curl command.

1

Fill in your day

Sleep, study hours, screen time, stress, and a few basics — twelve fields, all on one page.

2

We call the model

Your entry is sent straight to the /predict endpoint of your local FastAPI server.

3

Model does the math

A random forest, trained on lifestyle survey data, returns a single predicted score.

4

Read your result

Your score comes back circled, notebook-style, with a plain-language read of what it means.
